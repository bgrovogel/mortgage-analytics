Rising home f.values raise property taxes each year (limited by the appraisal cap for a homestead).

Defaults are long-run US averages: home f.values +4.3% a year (FHFA House Price Index, 1975–2025), home insurance +4.8% a year (GAO, average premium 2019–2024), HOA dues +4% (typically 3–5%). Local markets vary.
